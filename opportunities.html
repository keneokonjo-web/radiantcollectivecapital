The essentials

As published on our membership FAQs
Structure
Private co-investment group, not a registered fund. Investments are structured through Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs).
Focus
Primarily real estate (75%) and other vetted opportunities, across fintech, hospitality, AI and more.
Minimum
Currently $10,000 per opportunity, subject to change based on deal structure.
Horizon
Long-term opportunities, typically 3–5 years. Investments are typically illiquid.
Returns
Distributed pro rata to your capital contribution in each deal, after legal, administrative and other relevant expenses.
Participation
Completely optional per deal.

Please note. Like any private investment, there are risks including capital loss and illiquidity, and there is no guarantee of returns. Radiant does not give financial advice. Members should conduct their own due diligence and only invest if they are comfortable with this risk profile.
