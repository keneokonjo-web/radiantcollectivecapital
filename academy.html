Academy FAQs

When does the Academy open?

The Wealth Academy is coming in 2027. A launch date hasn’t been announced yet. Join the waitlist and you’ll hear first.

Do I need to be a Radiant member?

No. The Academy is open to anyone, whether or not you hold a Radiant membership.

Is the Academy included in the $500 membership?

No. The Academy is a separate purchase, priced at $995 (indicative and subject to change before launch). Membership is $500 a year and covers access to investment opportunities, the member network, the weekly market report and quarterly investment lounges.

How do the courses work?

They’re on demand and self-paced. There are around 12 signature courses that you can take in your own time.
